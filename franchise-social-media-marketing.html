Which Is Better for Multi-Location Businesses: Franchise Social Media Management or Franchise Social Media Marketing?

Franchise social media management and marketing are both crucial for businesses with several locations, but understanding when and how to apply each tactic can have a big influence on your company.

Each approach has advantages of its own and need to be used in accordance with your franchise's objectives.

Franchise Social Media Marketing

Creating and disseminating content across many social media channels is the foundation of franchise social media marketing (SMM), which aims to advertise your franchise company.

An effective franchise social media marketing plan can raise brand recognition, produce leads, and improve franchisee traffic.

Services for franchise social media marketing comprise:

  • creating social media content with the main marketing message in mind
  • Increasing brand recognition and visibility through a variety of social media platforms
  • building the brand's reputation positively on the internet
  • Developing relationships by interacting with consumers and future customers
Franchise Social Media Management

Having a presence on every social media network is crucial because the average user spends seven hours a month on these channels.

If you use franchise social media management software or outsource to a third-party firm, managing several networks is simple.

Software for managing social media for franchises comprises:

  • Publishing information and updates on social media sites on a frequent basis
  • Handling questions, messages, and comments on social media
  • Making original films, images, and other marketing content for social media
  • Creating a content calendar for purposeful content sharing and posting
