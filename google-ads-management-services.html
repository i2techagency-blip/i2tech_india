How Google Ad Manager Works

You have the freedom and control to design, oversee, and analyze each and every one of your advertising campaigns using Ad Manager. This is how the procedure is broken down:

  • Identify your ad units, or the places on your app or website where adverts will appear.
  • Create a distinct tag, or short code, for every ad unit that will be added to your website or application.
  • When a visitor visits the webpage or app, the ad tag makes a request to Ad Manager for an ad.
  • To handle your transactions with advertisers or purchasers of your ad inventory, create orders and line items.
  • Targeting campaigns to the ad unit will serve an advertisement for the request. The Ad Manager chooses the best ad to serve at the time of the request.
