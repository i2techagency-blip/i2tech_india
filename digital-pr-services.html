What is Digital PR?

Digital PR involves using online channels to increase brand awareness and enhance reputation. Unlike traditional PR, which focuses on print media and television, digital PR targets websites, blogs, influencers, and social media platforms. This approach not only boosts your online presence but also improves your SEO, driving more traffic to your website and helping you reach new audiences.
